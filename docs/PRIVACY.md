# Privacy Policy — Recursed Studios

Effective: 15 September 2026  
Contact: jeff@recursedstudios.com

Recursed Studios (“we”) operates creator tools and OAuth clients that connect to third-party platforms such as Patreon.

## What we collect

When you authorize our Patreon client, we receive only the data Patreon returns for the scopes you approved. That may include:

- account identity (name, user id, public profile)
- campaign metadata you administer
- membership and pledge status for campaigns you administer
- email, only if the `identity[email]` or `campaigns.members[email]` scope was granted

We do not scrape data outside those scopes.

## How we use it

We use authorized Patreon data to:

- confirm who signed in
- read campaign and member status for Recursed Studios tools
- refresh OAuth tokens so the connection stays valid

We do not sell this data. We do not use it for advertising.

## Storage

Access and refresh tokens are stored only as long as the integration is active. Tokens are not committed to public repositories.

## Sharing

We share data only with:

- Patreon, to complete OAuth and API requests
- you, when you ask us to show campaign or member information

We do not share data with unrelated third parties except if required by law.

## Retention and deletion

Revoke the app in your Patreon settings or email jeff@recursedstudios.com. We will delete stored tokens and cached profile data for that connection.
